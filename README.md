## The joke

81 characters, one line. Written for the judge's brief: it turns on a real
detail of office life (a resignation that is never replaced, just re-labelled),
lands its laugh in the last few words ("the one person who quit"), is clear on
one reading, and uses fresh wording rather than a joke already going round the
internet.
